Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I book a guide?

Search for your destination, browse available guides, and send a booking request.

How do I become a guide?

Create an account as a Guide, complete your profile, and wait for verification.

Is Guide Mate free?

Yes. Creating an account is free. Booking charges depend on the selected guide.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancellation depends on the guide's cancellation policy.
